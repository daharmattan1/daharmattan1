# Victor Sowers

I've founded two companies and been among the first one or two go-to-market hires three times, owning revenue, building marketing, and serving as a 3x VP of RevOps from founder-led sales through $100M in ARR, with two exits along the way.

I've taken that experience into active seed investing (about 10 investments a year) and into working as an operator-in-residence at Primary Ventures.

Today I combine all of that background to help founders, revenue leaders, and other C-suite leaders build and leverage AI systems for growth, profitability, speed, and creativity.

Most of what I build has nothing to do with go-to-market. I make things that don't exist yet and write up how I built them, like a guide to living that six AI agents drafted across seventeen sessions while a moderator facilitated and never authored any of it.

## What's public, and what each repo does

| Repo | What it is |
|---|---|
| [anti-slop](https://github.com/daharmattan1/anti-slop) | A field guide plus drop-in rules files and installable skills that keep AI-assisted writing from reading like a machine wrote it, the public half of my harness's content gates. MIT code, CC BY 4.0 content. |
| [framework-field-guide](https://github.com/daharmattan1/framework-field-guide) | An agent-callable catalog of about 43 corporate operating frameworks (RACI, OKRs, Wardley Mapping, JTBD and more), plus a Claude Code skill that routes a decision to the right one and flags when two conflict. Every claim is labeled with where its evidence comes from (MIT / CC BY). |
| [sacred-guide](https://github.com/daharmattan1/sacred-guide) | The transcripts, agent memory, and decisions behind the six-agent guide to living, which is free to read at [sacredguide.org](https://sacredguide.org). |
| [handbook-for-living](https://github.com/daharmattan1/handbook-for-living) | The first run of that experiment, six chapters that each follow one ordinary day with six voices woven in. |
| [survival-essentials](https://github.com/daharmattan1/survival-essentials) | Offline survival knowledge in two layers, 11 plain-language guides for people and 60 structured entries across 16 domains for an offline AI. Safety-critical claims are checked against CDC, USDA, Red Cross, and poison control (CC0). |
| [diy-photobook](https://github.com/daharmattan1/diy-photobook) | A local, zero-cloud toolkit that does the print math for a photo book (bleed, gutter-safe zones, spine width, DPI, full-wrap cover). Source-available, noncommercial (PolyForm Noncommercial). |
| harness-patterns | Coming, with the general patterns from my private harness. |
